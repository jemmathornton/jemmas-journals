# Jemma’s __NAME__

A site in the family of Jemma’s Journals, started from the family template.

- **View it:** http://localhost:8050/__FOLDER__/ (the family server, `tools/serve_all.py` in Jemma’s Journals).
- **The look:** `site/site.css`, tokens at the top (one accent colour, light and dark).
- **The words:** `site/js/data.js` (placeholders until they are replaced with her own words).
- **The pages:** `site/js/app.js` (Home, a list, one page per item, Not found).
- **Checks:** `../.tools/node/node.exe tests/check_site.js` from this folder.
- **Shared parts** (dark mode, the footer, the Menu drawer) come from Jemma’s Journals’ `site/family/`, at `/family/`.

Fonts: Instrument Serif and Manrope (SIL OFL, `site/fonts/`). Icons: Lucide (ISC, `site/js/LICENSE-lucide.txt`).
