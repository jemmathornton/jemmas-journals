## Broken

1. **World banner descriptions cut off or gone at 1280×800 (the laptop size).** *Where:* world pages, 1280×800. *What:* the banner has a fixed height. The new figures box is taller and the capitalised neighbourhood tags wrap onto 2–3 rows, so the description gets squeezed: 0 px tall on Willow Creek (no description at all), and a 12 px sliver (half a line, cut through the letters) on Oasis Springs, Windenburg, San Myshuno, Brindleton Bay, Del Sol Valley, Britechester and Ravenwood. On Willow Creek the title is pushed up against the frame. Before the restyle only 3 worlds lost it (Oasis Springs, Windenburg, San Myshuno); now it’s 8. Fine at 1600×930 and on the phone, which the commit fixed with `height:auto`. *Evidence:* `w-willow-creek_1280_light.jpg`, `w-britechester_1280_light.jpg`; measured description heights for all 25 worlds, old and new. *Fix:* give the laptop the same treatment as the phone (`.hero.mapped{height:auto;min-height:…}`), or keep the fixed height but make the tags and figures smaller below about 900 px tall. Add a site test that the world description is at least one line tall at 1280×800.

2. **Dark mode: “Member”, “Heir” and club “Member” badges can’t be read.** *Where:* Sim pages’ family tree and groups (Bella Goth), dynasty and club pages, dark mode. *What:* the badges use fixed dark text `#1A1F1C` on a background mixed from `--surface`, which is near-black in dark mode: contrast 1.04 (Member), 1.79 (Heir), 1.52 (club member). They show as empty gold outlines. This was already there before the restyle (same values with the old CSS) but it’s still broken. *Evidence:* `full/s-bella-goth_1280_dark.jpg`, `NEW-bella-dark-groups.jpg`. *Fix:* `color:var(--ink)` for `.badge.r-member`, `.r-heir`, `.c-member` (keep the dark text only on the solid-gold `.r-head`).

3. **Dark mode: the calendar’s selected “All worlds” chip is white text on a near-white chip.** *Where:* Calendar, World filter, dark mode. *What:* `.cal .wchip.on` falls back to `background:var(--ink)` (light in dark mode) with `color:#fff`. Also there before the restyle. *Evidence:* `full/calendar_1280_dark.jpg` (computed: text #FFF on #ECE7F3). *Fix:* `color:var(--paper)` for the fallback, or `--on` set from `--ink`.

## Should fix

4. **World titles in gold on bright maps are hard to read, now on 13 worlds, and darker than before in light mode.** *Where:* map banners. *What:* the known Britechester problem applies to every world with a one-word name, because the whole title is the gold `<em>`: Newcrest, Windenburg, Selvadorada, StrangerVille (gold on red rock), Sulani, Glimmerbrook, Britechester, Henford-on-Bagley, Copperdale, Tomarang, Ravenwood, Nordhaven, Ondarion. The restyle also changed the light-mode `--brass` from #C9A04A to #A47A24, so map titles in light mode are now a darker, duller gold (computed rgb 190,159,97) than in dark mode or before (216,187,125). Spaced capitals in a thin serif make it worse. *Evidence:* `brite-light.jpg` vs `brite-dark.jpg` vs `OLD-brite-light.jpg`; `full/w-strangerville_1600_light.jpg`. *Fix:* on `.hero.mapped` set the title gold back to the brighter shade (`.hero.mapped h1 em{color:#D8BB7D}` or the dark-mode brass); for one-word names, keep the title white with no gold, or add a darker local scrim behind the title. Her choice how it looks (see question A).

5. **Laptop neighbourhood grid leaves an empty column at 1600 wide (her window).** *Where:* world pages, 1600×930. *What:* the card grid minimum went from 250 to 240 px, so at 1600 the grid makes 4 columns. Worlds with 2–3 neighbourhoods (StrangerVille, Mt. Komorebi, Britechester…) show narrow cards with a blank quarter on the right; before, 3 cards filled the row. Narrow cards also wrap neighbourhood names in capitals (“STRANGERVILLE / PLAZA”). *Evidence:* `full/w-strangerville_1600_light.jpg` vs `OLD-strangerville-cards.jpg`. *Fix:* use `auto-fit` for the neighbourhood grid, or put the minimum back at 250 px for neighbourhood cards only.

6. **Sidebar “Not owned” worlds are fainter than before.** *Where:* sidebar, light mode. *What:* Forgotten Hollow, Tartosa and the others (some are links to their households) are 2.65:1 against the lilac, the “Not owned” label 2.18:1, the counts 2.07:1; dark mode 3.9:1. Before, on the dark purple sidebar, they were about 4:1. Her list already has an open **Decide** asking whether greyed sidebar text should be stronger, and the restyle made it weaker. *Evidence:* `home_1280_light.jpg` (sidebar bottom), sweep contrast results. *Fix:* raise the 45% / 35% mixes to about 65% / 60% (≈4.5:1), or answer her open Decide item first.

7. **Household cards use the lot-size mark for the home’s name.** *Where:* Households list, “Who lives here” on lot pages. *What:* the new dimension mark (|— 30 × 20 —|) is drawn round whatever is in `.size`, and on household cards that’s the lot name (“|— Oakenstead —|”), which reads like a measurement. The type tag also says “HOUSEHOLD” where lot cards name a lot type. *Evidence:* `hh-cards-1280.jpg`, `full/l-willow-creek--streamlet-single_1280_light.jpg`. *Fix:* draw the brackets only on lot sizes (e.g. `.listing:not(.hhcard) .photo .size::before`), or give the household card’s home name its own class.

8. **Search box focus is hard to see.** *Where:* sidebar search, both modes. *What:* `.search input:focus{outline:none;border-color:var(--brass)}` only turns a 1 px border gold, which barely shows on the white field. Every other control has the 2 px gold ring. *Evidence:* `focus-search_1280_light.jpg`. *Fix:* drop `outline:none` (the global `:focus-visible` ring then applies), or add `box-shadow:0 0 0 2px var(--brass)`.

9. **Phone Worlds drawer: keyboard and screen reader (unchanged by the restyle).** *Where:* any page, ≤900 px. *What:* Escape doesn’t close the drawer (it only clears the search). While the drawer is closed, Tab still walks through every sidebar link off-screen (the second Tab lands on “← Jemma’s Journals” at x = −286). Opening it doesn’t move focus into it. The button has no `aria-expanded`. Clicking the scrim or a link closes it correctly. *Evidence:* drawer.js output (scratchpad); `drawer-open_390_light.jpg`. *Fix:* set `inert` on `.side` while closed on phones, toggle `aria-expanded`, close on Escape and return focus to the Worlds button.
