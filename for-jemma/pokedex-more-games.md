# Your Pokédex with more games (22 Nov)

Ideas only. Nothing in the Pokédex has changed. The full notes and sources are in `research/pokedex-more-games.md`.

## The idea in short

- **One Pokédex for all your Pokémon.** Pokémon GO stays exactly as it is. Other games, and Pokémon HOME, get added alongside it.
- **Your GO ticks are never touched.** Each game gets its own ticks file next to GO’s, so nothing can get lost.
- **One game at a time on screen.** You pick the game, and the sidebar shows only that game’s pages. Costumes, backgrounds, events, What’s on and Looking for stay with GO.
- **A calm Home page.** Home could become a short shelf of your games, each with its cover and an “Open …” link. No Pokémon pictures on it.
- **Each game has its own boxes.** GO keeps all 13. HOME might have just Registered, Shiny, and Female/Male. Legends: Z-A might have Caught, Shiny, Alpha and Mega. The tabs at the top follow whichever game you’re in.
- **Pokémon names, numbers and Pokédex entries** would come from a free, open list of every game’s Pokédex (PokeAPI), word for word. Pictures would be the games’ own, like now.

## What you’d choose

The first option in each list is my suggestion (not decided). You’d try each one in the running Pokédex before choosing.

1. **Which games:** GO and HOME first · GO and the console game you play now · several at once · not yet
2. **How to switch game:** a small switch under “Jemma’s Pokédex” in the sidebar · each game as its own section in the sidebar · games listed on Home only · tabs along the top
3. **Home page:** a shelf of your games’ covers · the region maps with each game under its region · today’s Home kept for GO, plus a small Games page · a plain list of words
4. **Pictures on Home:** game covers · region maps · none
5. **Pokémon HOME:** its own Pokédex with ticks · a “where they live” view showing which games you have each Pokémon in · both
6. **HOME’s main box means:** you have it in HOME now · you’ve ever registered it · two boxes for both
7. **Forms in other games:** as in GO (regional forms separate, your form-by-form list, the rest on one card) · every form separate · regional forms only
8. **Pictures for other games:** GO’s pictures, plus HOME’s only where GO has none · HOME’s pictures for every game except GO · each game’s own pictures
9. **Fast tick:** GO only · in every game
10. **Addresses:** GO’s stay as they are and other games get their own · every game gets its own, and the old GO ones still work

## The steps

1. **You tell me** which games you play, and pick from the list above.
2. **Make room for more games**, with only GO in it at first: the switch and the new Home, as a Try switch for you to click through. *Small to medium job.*
3. **Add the full list of every Pokémon** (all 1,025, with their forms). *Medium job.*
4. **Add your first new game** (HOME, or the one you play most). *Medium job.*
5. **Optional: the “where they live” view** across your games. *Medium job.*
6. **Add more games**, each a small job once the first is done.

## Things to know

- More pictures would make the Pokédex folder bigger: about 40–70 MB if every game used HOME’s pictures. Starting with GO’s pictures avoids most of that.
- “Available” in a new game would mean “in that game’s own Pokédex”, the same way the games and HOME count it.
- The next new games (Pokémon Winds and Waves) come out in 2027. Pokémon Champions has nothing to collect.
